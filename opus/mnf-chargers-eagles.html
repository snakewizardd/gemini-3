KEY STORYLINES

🏆
Defending Champs Struggling
The Eagles are the defending Super Bowl champions but have lost two straight games, putting pressure on Jalen Hurts and the offense to turn things around on Monday night.
🎯
Hurts & Brown Connection
Jalen Hurts and A.J. Brown "haven't been on the same page for most of the season," but Brown has found his rhythm with at least seven catches in three straight games and three touchdowns in his last two.
📊
Playoff Implications - Chargers
The Chargers are in sixth place in the AFC. A win jumps them ahead of Buffalo for the No. 5 seed. A loss drops them completely out of the playoff picture. High stakes in Inglewood.
🦅
Eagles NFC East Control
Philadelphia currently holds the No. 3 seed in the NFC with control of the NFC East division. They're looking to maintain their position despite recent struggles.
🛡️
Elite Chargers Defense
The Chargers defense ranks 3rd-best in the NFL, allowing just 275.3 total yards per game. Can they contain Hurts, Barkley, and the Eagles playmakers?
⚠️
Eagles Statistical Concerns
Despite their record, the Eagles rank 24th in total offense (304.8 YPG) and 23rd in total defense (347.2 yards allowed). Something has to give against a top Chargers D.
