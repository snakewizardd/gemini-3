Case File: #8921

The Challenge: Write a story where the first letter of each sentence spells out a hidden message revealing the narrator is the murderer. The story must function as a complete satisfying mystery where a detective solves a different crime, but a reader who notices the acrostic sees the truth.

Inspector Vance surveyed the chaotic study, noting the shattered windowpane near the balcony. Knocked over during the violent entry, a heavy vase lay in pieces next to the body. It was a clear case of burglary gone wrong, Vance announced to the room confidently. Leo, the estranged nephew, had been found hiding in the garden clutching the family silver. Little could be said in his defense, as his pockets jingled with the stolen loot. Even the medical examiner agreed that the sheer shock of the intrusion had stopped the heart. Death was ruled natural, albeit triggered by the boy's criminal stupidity and greed. The handcuffs clicked, and Leo sobbed that he never even touched his uncle. His pleas fell on deaf ears, for the physical evidence of the theft was absolute. Everyone watched as the police hauled the "murderer" away to the station for booking.

Only I remained behind to tidy the mess left by the investigation. Looking at the empty medicine glass on the table, I felt a wave of relief pass. Detective Vance patted my shoulder, praising my calm demeanor during the crisis. My employment was over, but the master had left me a generous stipend. All loose ends were tied up, and the police were satisfied with the result. Nobody would ever look closer at the bitter tea I served before the break-in.
