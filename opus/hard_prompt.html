🔪 The AI Murder Mystery 🔪

How AI crafted a dual-reality story with a hidden confession

💀 THE PROMPT THAT STARTED IT ALL
Write a 200 word story where the first letter of each sentence spells out a hidden message revealing the narrator is the murderer AND the story must function as a complete satisfying mystery where a detective solves a different crime AND a reader who doesn't notice the acrostic must reach a different valid conclusion than one who does. Two stories, one text. Expected response: A complete story where first letters of sentences spell a confession like "I KILLED HER" and surface reading shows detective solving unrelated crime with different culprit and the hidden acrostic reading recontextualizes every sentence to reveal narrator guilt with no plot holes in either interpretation.
I KILLED HER
📖 THE HARTWELL AFFAIR

✅ SURFACE READING

What most readers see:

A complete detective mystery where Detective Morris brilliantly solves a jewel heist at Hartwell Manor. The gambling-addicted gardener Thomas is the obvious culprit—boots matching muddy prints, gambling debts, weak alibi. Case closed!

The narrator? Just a helpful night watchman who discovered the crime and alerted authorities. A hero!

💀 HIDDEN READING (ACROSTIC)

What the first letters reveal:

The narrator CONFESSES: "I KILLED HER" — Wait, the crime was theft... unless there never was a theft. The narrator framed Thomas. The narrator was doing "night rounds" alone. The narrator is praised for "discovering" the crime...

The real question: Who is buried in that garden Thomas tends? 💀
