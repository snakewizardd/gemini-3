- The goal is altered state, trance, and consciousness induction
- It should thump and bump, but with purpose

## Music Direction

- Key: `E Aeolian`
- Tempo: `132 BPM`
- Core aesthetic: `hypnotic techno propulsion, ritual atmosphere, engineered transcendence`
- Instrumentation spine: `dominant sub bass, heavy kick, rolling toms, metallic percussion, evolving synth pulse, breath-like pad, sparse mantra vox`
- Harmony direction: `natural minor gravity, circular tension, ritual descent and lift`

## Suggested Structure

1. **Invocation**
   Start with distant pad, filtered pulse, and a low ritual rumble. Introduce the sense that something is about to descend into the body.

2. **Descent**
   Bring in kick and sub with authority. The bass should not wobble randomly. It should feel like a controlled pressure field.

3. **Trance Engine**
   Lock the groove. Add metallic hats, tom movement, and a repeating motif that feels ceremonial rather than poppy.

4. **Expansion**
   Open the stereo image. Let the spiritual layer arrive here: breath, mantra, harmonic bloom, spectral lift, sacred geometry in sound.

5. **Peak State**
   Keep the kick and bass relentless, but let the top end feel luminous. This should be the consciousness-induction zone.

6. **Return**
   Strip elements away without collapsing the spell. End like a ritual closing, not a radio outro.
