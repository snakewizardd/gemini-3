## Research Organization

### Persona Families

**1. CP (Conscious Protagonist) Family** - Balanced, identity-driven
- `cp.html` → `cp2.html` → `cp3.html` (core variants)
- `cpmusic.html`, `cpmusic2.html` (audio integration)

**2. Husky Family** - Aggressive, momentum-driven
- `husky.html` → `husky2.html` → ... → `husky9.html` (iterative refinement)
- `husky_apex.html` (combined best)
- `husky_opus.html`, `husky_opus2.html` (audio + persona)

**3. Experimental/Specialized**
- `dda_arena.html`, `dda_gauntlet.html` (multi-scenario testing)
- `dda_superhuman_puzzle.html`, `dda_superlearner.html` (capability exploration)
- `unity_of_all_beings.html` (collective intelligence)

### Research Questions Addressed

- **Persona Design**: How do different personality parameters affect decision trajectories?
- **Aggression vs. Stability**: What's the optimal balance between momentum and caution?
- **Audio Integration**: Can DDA agents meaningfully control musical synthesis?
- **Multi-Agent Dynamics**: How do multiple DDA agents interact or compete?
- **Superhuman Performance**: Can DDA-driven agents solve complex problems?
- **Recursive Consciousness**: Can agents modeled after DDA recursively improve themselves?
